BUSINESS MEMO

To: Direct to Consumer Strategic Alignment Committee
From: Analytics Team
Subject: Core Investigative Measures Prior to Campaign Launch

**1. Resolve Underlying Operational Failures First**: 
 Before spending marketing capital on broad promotional discounts, we must guarantee that we are not trying to fix operational problems with marketing band-aids.
 For example, if a customer has an active, unresolved support ticket regarding a damaged package or broken checkout, sending them a 10% coupon will feel tone-deaf and ineffective.
 The recommended action is to programmatically suppress automatic marketing retention outreach for any customer with an Open or Escalated support issue and route them to 
 a priority customer service recovery queue instead.

**2. Establish Financial Guardrails Against Margin Leakage**
 Blindly offering discounts to every high-probability churn profile can destroy profit margins.
 Customers categorized as Champions often experience normal gaps between orders but still have high intent.
 Incentivizing them wastes budget on purchases they would have made anyway. The recommended action is to restrict cash-equivalent promotions to At-Risk and
 Price-Sensitive cohorts while applying experiential rewards like early product access to top-tier groups.

**3. Verify Data Pipeline Synchronization**
  The backend CRM tool must be fully prepared to handle the predictive model's outputs. The recommended action is to perform an end-to-end integration test
 between our data tables and the CRM delivery nodes to ensure incoming customer scores trigger targeted campaigns without delays.
